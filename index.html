Welcome to Lesotho

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain kingdom located in Southern Africa. It is known for its stunning landscapes, traditional culture and friendly people.

From breathtaking waterfalls to high mountain passes, Lesotho offers visitors memorable experiences and beautiful places to explore.
