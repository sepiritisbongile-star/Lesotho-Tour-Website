About the Kingdom of Lesotho

Discover our history, culture and beautiful heritage.

Our History

Lesotho is a landlocked country surrounded by South Africa. It was established under the leadership of King Moshoeshoe I, who united different communities during the nineteenth century.

Lesotho gained independence from British rule on 4 October 1966. Today, it remains a kingdom with a rich history and strong national identity.

Basotho Culture and Traditions

Traditional Clothing

The Basotho blanket is an important part of traditional clothing. It represents cultural identity and is worn during different occasions.

Traditional Food

Basotho traditional food includes papa, motoho, moroho and other locally prepared meals.

Music and Dance

Traditional music and dance play an important role in celebrations and cultural gatherings.

Our Natural Beauty

Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is famous for its high mountains and spectacular landscapes. Its entire territory lies at an elevation of at least 1,400 metres.

Natural Attractions

The country offers beautiful rivers, waterfalls, valleys and mountain scenery that attract visitors throughout the year.
